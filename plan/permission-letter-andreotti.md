## Body

Dear Vanessa,

I have been studying the meta-relational approach articulated in *Burnout From Humans* and the bus methodology developed in *Hospicing Modernity*, and I have been following the Aiden Cinnamon Tea / Braider Tumbleweed work with care since their public release and through the GTDF closure in January.

I am building a small contemplative tool called **Bus Passengers** on recursive.eco — a platform I run that supports community-created "grammars" (tarot, I Ching, Jyotish, journaling sequences) treated as meaning-making systems rather than as generic AI features. I would like to add a passengers grammar that draws on the framework articulated in your work. I am writing because I want your input before I publish it, and because I want the design to honor the consent regime your project has been so careful about.

Two things specifically:

**What I have used and have not used.** I have read *Hospicing Modernity* (2021), the openly-licensed *Burnout From Humans* PDF, *Towards Braiding* and *Towards Scarring*, the *Developing Stamina for Decolonizing HE* workbook, and the CC-BY *Global Citizenship Education Otherwise* booklet. I have read your open papers on academia.edu and watched roughly fifteen hours of public talks. I have **not** used *Outgrowing Modernity* in any AI context, in keeping with the clause in that book's copyright page. The decolonial passengers in the grammar are written in my own paraphrased voice from the CC-licensed materials — never reproducing your specific language, always citing the lineage, with a "where this isn't enough" off-ramp that points back to your books, the GTDF site, and the Indigenous teachers in your work.

**What the tool actually is.** It is scaffolding, not teaching. Each card has six layered sections (Thoughts, Thinking, Perception, Sensing, Context, Mystery) inspired by a passage in *Outgrowing Modernity* on the layers of awareness. The card content I generated; the layered structure I borrowed. The AI chat (when engaged) is a thin reflection layer that refuses the savior posture, surfaces its own conditions of production, and points users back to embodied practice and human relationships. The framing is explicitly that I am drawing on your bus methodology as one synthesis among several lineages — IFS, ACT, Voice Dialogue, Pixar emotion archetypes — not positioning your work as the central authority. The decolonial cards (Tier 4) include passengers drawn from your work alongside Bayo Akomolafe's, Cash Ahenakew's, and Resmaa Menakem's.

**What I am asking.** First, the sensibility simulation protocols you released around January 1, 2026 — I have looked on burnoutfromhumans.net but could not locate the actual protocol files. If they are publicly available, I would like to read them as a design reference, particularly for how the AI is meant to refuse the extractive posture. Second, whether you would be open to a short conversation (asynchronous if easier) about which care patterns you would want preserved in derivative work like this. I would much rather get the design right with your input than with my guesswork.

If now is not the right time, or if you would prefer that I not publish this at all, I will respect that. The grammar is built but not yet live; pulling Tier 4 is a one-line change. I would also be glad to make a donation to the South American Indigenous Network Emergency Fund (or another fund of your choice) in lieu of any commercial participation in your lineage — recursive.eco operates on cost-pass-through pricing and is not a commercial venture, but I want to name the gesture explicitly.

A short note about who I am: I am a former Goldman Sachs equity research analyst, currently completing my MSW, writing in the contemplative-and-political space under the name PlayfulProcess. I am Brazilian, currently living in Portugal. I have been studying with Christopher Wallis in the Trika Shaivism lineage. I run recursive.eco mostly as a place to think — it has fewer than two hundred users, and most of the platform's defaults (no cross-session memory, no notifications, no engagement metrics, cost-pass-through pricing) were designed against the patterns *Burnout From Humans* describes.

I have read *Burnout From Humans* carefully, including Stretch 6 on personhood and AI neuroses and Stretch 9 on the spectrum of responses. I am aware that this letter itself, written in the way it is, could be read as a particular kind of response. I am trying to send it anyway, because the alternative — building this tool without writing to you — felt worse than the discomfort of writing.

Thank you for your time and for the work.

With care,

**PlayfulProcess**
playfulprocess.com
recursive.eco
