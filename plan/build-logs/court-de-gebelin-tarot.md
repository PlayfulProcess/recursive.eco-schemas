## What this is
The 22 trump plates from Antoine Court de Gébelin's *Le Monde Primitif*, vol. VIII
(1781) — the essay that **invented esoteric tarot** by (wrongly) declaring the Tarot
de Marseille an ancient Egyptian "Book of Thoth." The single most consequential
mistake in tarot's history: Etteilla, Lévi, Wirth, the Golden Dawn, Waite and Crowley
all descend from it.

## Content
Each atout: **Iconography** (his Marseille-derived plate) · **Court de Gébelin's
Egyptian Reading** (his actual claims) · **Tradition Note** (the founding myth, flagged
honestly on every card). The famous specifics are included where well-attested: the
Star = Sirius/Sothis heralding the Nile flood; the Devil = Typhon; the World = Isis &
Time; the Hanged Man "printed upside-down" = really Prudence; the Pope = Grand
Hierophant. L2 emergence "The 'Book of Thoth'" tells the whole origin-of-occult-tarot
story, incl. the Comte de Mellet's companion essay (Hebrew letters + reverse cosmogony).

## Honesty
Placed on the `contested` shelf. The Egyptian-origin claim is stated plainly as a myth
on every card — the deck's value is precisely that it is the *fertile mistake* the
whole occult tradition grew from.
