## What this is
The Minchiate — the 97-card Florentine expansion of tarot. The fullest historical
tarot, built within its own tradition.

## Tradition features captured
- **Five Papi** (replacing Popess/Empress/Emperor/Pope/Love; the Popess dropped).
- **Complete seven virtues**: cardinal (Temperance, Fortitude, Justice) + Prudence
  + theological (Faith, Hope, Charity). L2 emergence "The Seven Virtues — Complete".
- **Four elements** (Fire, Water, Earth, Air) as trumps.
- **Twelve zodiac signs** in Minchiate's own order (Libra first); each card carries
  its element/mode/ruler.
- **Five Arie** (Star, Moon, Sun, World, Le Trombe = the Last Judgement) — the
  supreme cards.
- 56 suited cards (Batons/Coins/Cups/Swords × 10 pips + 4 courts); notes on the
  centaur knights and female pages of Cups/Coins.
