The open grammar commons

A public library of grammars — structured symbolic systems (tarot, the I Ching, sacred texts, mythology, children's stories) rendered as JSON anyone can read, fork, or feed to the recursive viewer at recursive.eco. Everything here is CC-BY-SA-4.0. Below are interactive ways to explore it.

Interactive visualizations

Self-contained explorers — open in any browser, no install.

☯
I Ching The Leibniz Binary Hypercube All 64 hexagrams on an 8×8 trigram grid — toggle inverse / complement / nuclear / King Wen / Zagua relationships, Eight-Palaces coloring, and line-change neighbors, with the classical Chinese line texts.
⊞
Any grammar The Grammar Playground Paste any grammar.json URL (GitHub links auto-convert) and explore its items — filter by level and category, draw random cards, read every section.
